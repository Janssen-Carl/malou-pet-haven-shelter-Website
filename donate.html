About Us

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe haven for pets in need, offering them love, care, and a chance for a better life. We strive to find forever homes for our furry friends and promote responsible pet ownership in our community.

Our History

Malou Pet Haven Shelter was founded in 2020 with the goal of rescuing and rehabilitating abandoned and abused pets. Over the years, we have grown into a dedicated team of volunteers and staff who work tirelessly to provide the best care for our animals. Our shelter has become a beacon of hope for pets in need, and we continue to expand our efforts to make a positive impact in our community.
